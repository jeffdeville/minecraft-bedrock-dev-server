# Your workspace

`packs/` is your add-on: `kid_bp` is the code half, `kid_rp` is the
pictures-and-names half, and both already work. `lessons/` is what you read
in the side panel; start with `00-tour`. `assets/` holds pictures the lessons
ask you to copy into place. Files save themselves a second after you stop
typing, and the status bar at the bottom says **live** when your server has
your latest change.
